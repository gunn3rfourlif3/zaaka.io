The studio

Zaaka is a design and engineering studio in Johannesburg. We take products from an idea to something running in production, and build the websites and brands around them.

What we actually do

Most studios sit on one side of a line: they design and hand over, or they build what someone else designed. We do both, because the gap between the two is where projects lose their quality — the detail that made a design good is exactly the detail that gets dropped when it is being interpreted by someone who was not in the room.

That means we take on fewer projects than an agency of the same size, and go deeper into each one. If you need twelve people on something next week, we are the wrong studio. If you want the person designing your product to be the person building it, we are worth a conversation.

What we have built

Locare is a white-label property management platform that rental agencies run their business on — leasing, rent collection reconciled into a double-entry ledger, trust accounting with owner payouts, maintenance ticketing, and branded apps for tenants and landlords. It handles other people’s money, which sets the standard for everything else we make.

Uberfi is a profit copilot for rideshare drivers: it reads an incoming trip request, prices it against the driver’s real running costs, and returns a keep-or-reject verdict inside the fifteen seconds they have to decide.

Both are in the work, written up properly — including the decisions we would defend and the ones we would make differently.

How we think about the work

Own what you paid for

Hosting, licences, analytics, source code and design files go in your name from the start. Nothing we build depends on us continuing to exist. Studios that make leaving difficult are managing a risk that good work would have removed.

Say the hard thing early

If a brief asks for the wrong thing, if a budget will not stretch to the scope, if a deadline is not real — we would rather say so in week one and be argued with than agree politely and deliver a disappointment.

Measure what you claim

Fast and accessible are easy to promise and easy to check. We run the numbers before calling anything finished, and we will show you them rather than asking you to take our word for it.

Where we are

Johannesburg, working remotely with clients in South Africa, the UK and Europe. The time zone helps more than it hurts: an hour ahead of London and an hour behind most of Europe means a full working day of overlap rather than a handover window.
